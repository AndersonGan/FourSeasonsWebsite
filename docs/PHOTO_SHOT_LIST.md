# Photo shot list

Goal: replace illustrations in key spots with **real** photos of **real portions**, while keeping
the illustrated identity. Shoot on a weekday afternoon before dinner service.

**Light:** warm, directional window light (side-lit, ~45°). No flash. **Surfaces:** the restaurant's
actual tables and plates. **Formats:** shoot each dish both landscape (3:2) and portrait (4:5); leave
breathing room for cropping. **Export:** full-resolution JPEG; the site converts to AVIF/WebP.
